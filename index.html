Small interactive programs written for the Statistics course.

  1. HMWK 1 Scalar multiplication on a toy curve Elliptic-curve scalar multiplication on the toy curve y² = x³ + 7 (mod 17), with a slowed-down animation and a statistical exploration.
  2. HMWK 2 Twelve words to an Ethereum address BIP-39, BIP-32, BIP-44, secp256k1, Keccak-256 and EIP-55 computed step by step in the browser, with their roles, history and statistical properties.
  3. HMWK 3 ElGamal on secp256k1 and the Caesar cipher The same message encrypted with a toy elliptic-curve scheme and with the Caesar cipher, compared and attacked.
